Privacy policy

Your reading is yours alone.

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Here is exactly what the app stores, what it connects to, and why.

Effective 15 October 2026Applies to the Ultimate Prophecy Bible Study app and this website

01The short version

We don’t collect your data.

Ultimate Prophecy Bible Study has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you read, highlight and note stays on your own device. We never see it, and we have nothing to sell.

This policy explains what the app stores on your device, the few times it connects to the internet, and the permissions it asks for. “We” means Ultimate Prophecy, the publisher of the app. “The app” means the Ultimate Prophecy Bible Study mobile app. “The site” means this website.

02Information we collect

None. The app does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or give us a name, email address, phone number or location. We run no servers that receive information from the app, and we do not use analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting services.

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to sell, rent, share or disclose.

03What stays on your device

To work the way you expect, the app saves some things locally on your phone or tablet, in the app’s private storage:

StoredWhy
Reading position and progressSo the app opens where you stopped and shows your progress.
Highlights, notes and bookmarksThe marks you make while reading, so you can find them again.
Chain study and quiz progressSo a study you started can be resumed.
SettingsTheme, type size and similar preferences.
Downloaded audio and cached coversChapters you choose to save, and cover images kept so they don’t reload each time.

This information never leaves your device through the app. Removing the app, or clearing its storage in your system settings, deletes all of it.

04When the app uses the internet

The Bible text, cross-references, commentary index, chain studies, tables, maps and daily verses are bundled inside the app and work offline. The app connects to the internet only when you ask for something that lives online:

  • Audiobooks and chapter audio. Audio and cover images are streamed or downloaded from servers operated by third parties, including sathyavachanam.com and manna.amazingfacts.org.
  • “Read in full” links and study guides. Tapping one opens the page in your browser, for example on egwwritings.org or bibleuniverse.com.

When your device contacts one of these services, that service can see technical details that any website sees, such as your IP address and the time of the request. We do not control these services and do not receive that information. Their own privacy policies apply.

05Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhat for
PhotosOnly if you choose to set a verse on a photo of your own. You pick one image through the system photo picker. The card is made on your device and the photo is not uploaded anywhere.
Notifications (Android 13 and later)To show the media controls for audio on the lock screen and notification shade. The app does not send promotional notifications.
Background audio and wake lockSo audio keeps playing when the screen is off.
InternetTo stream audio and open links, as described above.
Storage (Android 9 and older)To save a chapter’s audio into your Music folder when you ask. Newer Android versions need no permission for this.

You can change or withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings. The app keeps working without them, apart from the feature that needs it.

06Sharing

When you tap Share on a verse or a verse card, the app hands the text or image to your device’s share sheet. You choose where it goes. What the receiving app or person does with it is governed by their policies. The app shares nothing on its own.

07Children

The app is suitable for readers of all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It has no accounts, chat, ads or purchases that could expose a child to a third party through the app. Links that open a website take the reader out of the app, and we encourage parents to supervise that.

08Security and retention

Because your marks and settings are stored only on your device, their security is that of your device. Keep your phone locked and updated. We keep no copy, so we cannot restore them if you remove the app or lose the device, unless your phone’s own backup does so.

09Your choices and rights

Laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR and California’s CCPA give people rights to access, correct, delete or move their personal data. We hold none about you, so there is nothing to request from us. You are always in control locally: delete a mark in the app, remove saved audio, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall it.

If you believe we hold information about you, or you have a question about this policy, write to us at thrividhadoothu@gmail.com.

10Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, for example by adding a feature that connects to a new service, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches you. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
